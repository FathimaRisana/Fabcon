Central Battery System

Centralized Emergency Lighting & Battery Solutions

← Back to Services

Fabcon provides Central Battery System (CBS) solutions for emergency lighting and life-safety applications in commercial, industrial and infrastructure facilities.

A central battery system provides a centralized backup power source for emergency lighting and designated emergency loads, helping maintain illumination during normal power failure.

Our Central Battery System Services

  • Central battery system supply
  • Central battery cabinets
  • Battery systems and battery banks
  • Emergency lighting power distribution
  • Central monitoring systems
  • Battery replacement
  • Battery inspection and testing
  • System installation
  • Cable and circuit termination
  • Functional testing
  • Testing and commissioning
  • Preventive maintenance
  • Troubleshooting and corrective maintenance

Applications

Central battery systems can be used for:

  • Commercial buildings
  • Data centers
  • Hospitals
  • Hotels
  • Shopping malls
  • Industrial facilities
  • Warehouses
  • Airports and transportation
  • High-rise buildings

Detailed Service Capabilities

Fabcon provides robust uninterruptible power supply (UPS) and battery systems designed to deliver continuous runtime and power conditioning for mission-critical industrial, medical, and data center facilities.

UPS & battery capabilities include:

  • Online double-conversion UPS systems
  • Industrial UPS installations (10kVA to 800kVA+)
  • UPS preventive maintenance and servicing
  • Battery bank replacement and capacity testing
  • Battery rack and cabinet fabrication
  • Automated and manual maintenance bypass switches
  • VRLA and high-density Lithium-ion battery integrations
  • Power monitoring, telemetry, and SNMP communications
  • Emergency electrical troubleshooting and repairs
  • Site Acceptance Testing (SAT) and commissioning
